Privacy Policy — GKF Kart Racing

Last updated: 11 November 2026

This is the English translation of the Italian privacy policy. If the two versions differ, the Italian version prevails.

1. Data controller

Cristi Scutaru, Alpignano (TO), Italy — e-mail: globalkartingplatform@gmail.com ("we" or "GKF").

2. What the app does

GKF Kart Racing is a kart racing game for phones. You race against the recorded times ("ghosts") of other players and against computer-controlled drivers. With SCENDI IN PISTA (GO TO THE TRACK) you race the 1VS8 races of the seasons: each season lasts 28 days and at the end the best players receive in-game rewards. You can also join a clan (a team) with other players. The game also works without a connection; the online part (seasons, leaderboards, ghosts, league, clans, backup of your progress) requires internet.

In-app purchases. Payment takes place entirely on Google Play. To deliver the content and verify the receipt, our server sends Google the receipt code (purchase token) and receives from Google the purchase status, the product and the order ID. We keep a fingerprint (hash) of the receipt code, the order ID, the product and the date, linked to your game ID: they are used to avoid delivering the same purchase twice and to give you NO ADS back on the same Google account. The Race Pass Premium is valid only for the current season and, if you reinstall the game, it comes back with the backup of your progress.

Crash reports. Crash reports (Unity Cloud Diagnostics) contain the phone model, the Android and game versions and the point in the code where the game stopped. They do not contain your driver name.

Notifications. If you allow them, the game schedules reminders on your phone (part ready, daily gift, clan events). The phone itself prepares and shows them, so we do not send data to anyone. You can turn them off at any time in the Android settings. The dots on the game buttons (for example on the NEGOZIO / SHOP when the daily gift is ready) are calculated on the phone.

We do not process precise location, contacts, photos or microphone data. We do not sell personal data.

4. Who receives the data (processors)

  • Unity Technologies (Unity Gaming Services). Services used: Authentication, also for the link with Google Play Games; Cloud Code, the server that calculates points and rewards and verifies purchases; Cloud Save, for ghosts, season data and backup; Leaderboards, for the season, track and clan leaderboards; Cloud Diagnostics. Servers are in the EU/USA, with standard contractual clauses. Privacy policy: https://unity.com/legal/privacy-policy
  • Google LLC. Services used: Google Play (distribution and in-app purchases); Google Play Developer API (receipt verification); Google Play Games (account link); Google AdMob (ads). Privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy
  • AdMob advertising partners show personalised ads only if you give your consent in the form shown when you first open the game. You can change your mind in the game's IMPOSTAZIONI (SETTINGS).

5. Data visible to other players

Your driver name, online and season points, position in the season leaderboards (AMATORIALE and PRO), league and your best races (ghosts), with the colours of your kart, suit and helmet, are visible to other players in the leaderboards and on the track. If you join a clan, all players see the clan, its members (driver name) and their best event laps; your clan teammates see the symbol reactions you send. Clans have no chat and no written messages: clan names are made only of words chosen from a list. Do not put personal information in your driver name.

Invite a friend and challenges. At the end of a race the INVITA UN AMICO (INVITE A FRIEND) button opens the phone's share menu (for example WhatsApp) with a message that you decide whether and to whom to send:

  • if you have a published record on that track, it is a challenge: the link contains your driver name, your anonymous game ID, the track and your time. Whoever receives it, and anyone it is forwarded to, can see this data and race against your ghost;
  • otherwise it is a simple invitation with the link to the game on Google Play, without any of your data.

You share the link yourself, with the app you choose, under that app's terms.

6. Minors

The game is intended for users aged at least 13, as the start screen also asks. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 14 beyond what is strictly necessary for the game to work. If you are a parent and want your child's data to be deleted, write to us.

7. Your rights

You can ask us for access, rectification, erasure, restriction, portability and object to processing (Arts. 15-22 GDPR) by writing to globalkartingplatform@gmail.com. You can lodge a complaint with the Italian Data Protection Authority (Garante per la protezione dei dati personali, www.garanteprivacy.it) or with the supervisory authority of the country where you live.

Deletion from the app. IMPOSTAZIONI → CANCELLA TUTTI I DATI (SETTINGS → DELETE ALL DATA) deletes:

  • the data on the phone;
  • your ghosts;
  • the backup of your progress;
  • the online account, together with its link to Google Play Games;
  • your membership of the clan.

It is immediate and permanent. If you also want your row (driver name and score) removed from leaderboards already published and the season data left on the server, write to us with your driver name: we remove them within 30 days. Only the purchase data that the law requires us to keep remain.

8. Security

Online data travels encrypted (HTTPS) and is stored on Unity's servers. Season points and rewards are calculated by the server, not by the phone. The key our server uses to verify purchases with Google is kept in Unity's services and is never in the app. On the phone, progress is saved in the app's private storage.

9. Changes

If we change this policy we update the date at the top and, for important changes, we tell you in the game.
