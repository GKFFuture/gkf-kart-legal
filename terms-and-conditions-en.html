3. Account, driver name and progress backup

Going online does not require registration: the App creates an anonymous player ID. If you have Google Play Games on your phone, the App links your Play Games account to that player. So, if you reinstall the App or change phone, signing in with the same Google account brings back your online profile. The App keeps an online backup of your progress. If the online copy and the copy on the phone differ, the App shows you both and you choose which one to keep; the other is not deleted immediately. If you delete your data from the App (IMPOSTAZIONI → CANCELLA TUTTI I DATI / SETTINGS → DELETE ALL DATA) you permanently lose progress, backup, consumable purchases, online points, season points and ghosts.

4. Content and fair play

Online races are run against times recorded by other players. The points of online races and seasons are calculated by our server, which automatically checks every race. A race does not count, or stays "under review" until we check it, in these cases:

  • the time is impossible for that track;
  • the same race is sent more than once;
  • races are run too many times in a short period or always against the same opponent;
  • the time is much better than your usual times without a ghost confirming it.

Times obtained with cheats or tampering may be removed, also from clan events and seasons. The account may be excluded from leaderboards, seasons and their rewards, and from clans. We decide, in good faith, what counts as tampering. Rewards delivered for races later found to be irregular may be taken back.

4-bis. Clans

The CLAN page opens from level 10. You can found a clan by paying 5,000 in-game credits, or join an existing clan for free. A clan has a maximum of 20 drivers; you can be in only one clan at a time and leave whenever you want. The clan name is made only of words chosen from a list in the game; emblem and colour are chosen from those offered. Whoever founds a clan does not become its owner and does not decide who joins: any player can join while there is room.

Clans have no chat and no written messages: teammates can only send each other symbol reactions (BRAVO, CAMPIONE, VELOCE).

Events. Every Wednesday and every weekend (Saturday and Sunday) there is a clan event on a track shown in the game. Every race finished on that track saves your best lap. In event races everyone races with the same stock kart of the track's category (for example GKF EVO SPRINT on the rental tracks): the game lends it to you only for the race and then removes it, without leaving any parts; your kart and your setups do not change. The clan time is the average of the 5 best laps of its drivers; a clan enters the leaderboard only if at least 3 drivers have raced. The fastest clan wins a reward of 20,000 credits and 100 in-game gems, split equally among its drivers who raced in the event. If you leave the clan, your event laps no longer count for that clan.

Clan rewards are virtual content (see section 5): they have no real value and do not give cash prizes. We may change event rules, tracks, times and rewards, remove times obtained unfairly, and disband or rename clans when needed to run the game or to enforce these terms.

4-ter. Invite a friend and challenges

At the end of a race you can use INVITA UN AMICO (INVITE A FRIEND): the game opens the phone's share menu (for example WhatsApp) with a message and a link. If you have a published record on that track, the message is a challenge: the link contains your driver name, your anonymous game ID, the track and your time. Otherwise it is an invitation with the link to the game on Google Play. You choose whether and to whom to send it; whoever receives the link can forward it to others.

Whoever accepts the challenge races against your ghost (your best race on that track, with the colours you chose for kart, suit and helmet). Challenges do not change the online ranking or season points and give the normal rewards of a race. Do not use your driver name or challenge messages to offend or harass other players.

4-quater. Seasons (SCENDI IN PISTA)

Duration and leaderboards. The 1VS8 online races of SCENDI IN PISTA (GO TO THE TRACK) are part of the seasons. Each season lasts 28 days; start and end are shown in the game (UTC time). There are two season leaderboards: AMATORIALE (AMATEUR) and PRO. You appear in the one of the league you race in.

When a race counts. Season points are earned based on your finishing position against the ghosts of other real players. A race counts only if:

  • there is at least one valid real player on the grid, that is with a ghost of the same game physics version on that track. Races only against computer drivers do not give season points;
  • you respect the game limits: a maximum number of counted races per day, a minimum pause between one counted race and the next, a maximum number of counted races against the same opponent on the same day.

End of season. At the end of the season points go back to zero; level, karts, parts and the rest of your progress remain yours.

Rewards. End-of-season rewards depend on your final position in your leaderboard and require a minimum number of counted races in the season, shown in the game (currently 10). At the moment:

Season rewards are virtual content (see section 5): they have no real value, cannot be converted into money and cannot be transferred. For future seasons we may change duration, leagues, points, limits and rewards, showing it in the game. We may cancel or recalculate a season in case of technical errors or cheating.

5-bis. Seasonal Race Pass

The Race Pass follows the seasons of SCENDI IN PISTA: each season lasts 28 days and start and end are shown in the game. By playing you earn stars that level up the pass; at every level reached you immediately receive the reward of the free track and, if you have Premium, also that of the Premium track.

Race Pass Premium. It is bought in the NEGOZIO (SHOP) with real money (in Italy €4.99; the price in your currency is shown by Google Play before you confirm) and is valid only for the current season:

  • it is not a subscription and does not renew automatically: for the next season, if you want, you buy it again;
  • if you buy it when the season has already started, you immediately also receive the Premium rewards of the levels you have already reached; the remaining days are shown in the game;
  • at the end of the season the pass starts again from level 1, both the free track and the Premium track. Rewards already received and exclusive colours won remain yours; levels not reached by the end of the season give no rewards.

Stars, levels and rewards of the pass may change from one season to the next, as shown in the game.
