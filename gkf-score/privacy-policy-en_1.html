Privacy Policy — GKF Score

Last updated: 13 November 2026

1. Data controller

Cristi Scutaru, Alpignano (TO), Italy — e-mail: globalkartingplatform@gmail.com (“we”).

2. What the app does

GKF Score is an arcade game for phones: you drive a kart through an endless tunnel, dodge obstacles and escape the “kart inspectors” to get the highest score. The game works offline. The online part (world leaderboards, weekly prizes, account linking) needs internet.

5. Data visible to other players

Your driver name, scores and leaderboard position are visible to all players. Don't put personal data such as your real full name, phone number or address in your driver name.

6. Children

The game is meant for users aged 13 and over. If you are younger than the age at which your country's law lets you consent on your own (14 in Italy, up to 16 in some EU countries), a parent's consent is required. If you think a child gave us data without permission, write to us and we will delete it.

7. Your rights

You can ask to access, correct or delete your data, restrict or object to its use, and receive it in a portable format (Arts. 15–21 GDPR), and you can withdraw consent to personalised ads at any time. Write to globalkartingplatform@gmail.com with your driver name: we reply within 30 days. You can also complain to your data protection authority (in Italy: Garante per la protezione dei dati personali).

Uninstalling the game deletes all data stored on your phone. To also delete your online profile (driver name, leaderboard scores, Google or Apple link) use SETTINGS > DELETE ONLINE DATA in the game, or write to us. In SETTINGS you will also find AD PREFERENCES to change your advertising consent.

8. Changes

If this policy changes we will update the date above and, for important changes, tell you in the game.
